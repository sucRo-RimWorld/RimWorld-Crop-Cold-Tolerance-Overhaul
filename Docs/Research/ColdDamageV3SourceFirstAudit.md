# CCTO V3 — empirical-source-first crop audit (2026-10-24)

**Status: evidence review, not A/D or variation approval.** This is the current *numeric-selection input* for the next five unapproved HP candidate plants. It extends the original [ColdDamageCalibration.md](../ColdDamageCalibration.md), [V2 predeclared targets](../ColdDamageBalanceV2Targets.md), original measured [W13 wheat survival](WinterWheatW13Deryabin24hSurvival.csv) and [M01 maize model identifiability](MaizeM01DegreeHourIdentifiability.csv). Four previously author-selected A/D crops (Tomato, Pumpkin, Cabbage, Onion) are out of scope and unchanged. All original numeric inputs of the five below remain **trial-only**, and their candidate CSV status is held for source/stage/endpoint reconciliation.

## Source-to-game mapping rule (before selecting numbers)

1. Extract **measured temperature, exposure duration, species/cultivar, biological tissue, age/stage, acclimation, and post-thaw observation time**. Quote observed **whole-living-plant survival** separately from leaf/electrolyte leakage, yield/fruit/crown viability. Never treat laboratory-hour survival as a universal RimWorld game-hour death curve.
2. Check whether `A` (cold injury starts) and `D` (full HP lethal degree-C game-hours) in the current source model can make **nonzero loss at the observed temperature**. If T>=A, CCTO always gives 0 HP damage **regardless of duration**; an altered D or individual variation cannot fix this.
3. Compare independent typical, severe and accumulated/seasonal outcomes, and state where **same-plant-state studies disagree with a single static curve**. The approved V3 model deliberately has **no extra acclimation, life-stage or cold-only HP recovery system**.
4. When published evidence is tissue-only, stage-exclusive or mutually incompatible, mark the numeric adoption **HOLD** instead of inventing A/D. A source-informed gameplay abstraction may be proposed later with a clearly selected **representative crop-state and acceptable deviation**, but is not experimentally fitted.

## Unavoidable identifiability and the recommended minimal game model

- **Do not introduce rice 5-day acclimation flags, wheat 7-day cold-hardening memory, seedling-adult tissue state, maize sigmoid dose or separate fruit/flower health as invented prerequisites.** Each is real agronomic biology but exceeds the accepted simple HP V3 framework. Explicitly designate a **representative gameplay state**, accept stated loss of fidelity, and use the existing A/D first.
- **Do not fit w=.20 globally** from experiment groups or cultivar survival differences: low-temperature conditioning, crop stage and genotype effects are not iid fixed Thing-ID spread. Preserve w=0 source default and w=.20 as separately unapproved per-plant trials.
- **Plausibility before gameplay calibration:** a) choose representative state; b) set physically appropriate onset *range*, distinguishing leaf injury vs death; c) seek multiple **same-state / same-cultivar / same-endpoint** time-temperature whole-plant curves. If absent, D is a **gameplay compromise**, not an empirically fitted LD100. d) cross-check ≥one mild and severe event plus repeated nights and real world/season crop exposure before proposing a numeric change. No new tests, builds, XML or CSV A/D adoption in this audit.
- **Priority to unblock CCTO generic crop choices:** (1) Rice and Wheat—acclimation models are demonstrably nonidentifiable with 2 static fields; choose generic non-acclimated versus hardened reference behavior without simulating hardening; (2) Maize—avoid fragile exact fits to two rounded 50% seedling endpoints, preserve gameplay robustness; (3) Potato and Strawberry—resolve what a normal RimWorld Plant's whole HP/harvest outcome should stand for. Other unapproved profiles may reuse this protocol without a mandatory one-paper-per-species rule.

The D-crossing estimate is only the first possible full HP-dose time if the hypothetical plant *could* have remained alive and not been harvested, assuming no capped-sampler under/overbilling or other damage. **All five show zero theoretical low-temperature HP injury during calendar days 0–19 on this particular source world-tile series**, but this is *not* a general statement about viable growing seasons, other biome/latitude, post-harvest survival or all new maps. They are independent world-context checks, not excuses to fit the wrong biological cohort or to treat legacy A/D as numerically accepted.
