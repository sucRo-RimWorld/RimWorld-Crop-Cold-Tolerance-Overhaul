[h1]Crop Cold Tolerance Overhaul (CCTO)[/h1]

CCTO is a [b]realism-focused mod that makes the cold response of RimWorld plants more closely reflect real differences in plant cold tolerance[/b].

It uses information on frost injury, chilling damage, overwintering ability, and lethal temperatures as references, then translates those differences into clear, practical gameplay thresholds.

In Vanilla, many plants use a shared rule that derives leafless and cold-death behavior from their minimum growth temperature. CCTO instead revises minimum growth temperatures plant by plant and gives supported plants either a fixed cold-death temperature or cold dormancy.

[h2]Key features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Realism-focused balance that emphasizes real differences in cold tolerance between plants
[*]Plant-specific minimum growth temperatures
[*]Fixed cold-death temperatures for plants that do not use dormancy
[*]Cold dormancy for selected perennial and overwintering plants
[*]Cold death or dormancy temperature shown in the plant Info Card
[*]Only cold-tolerance-related properties are changed. Yield, growth time, fertility, storage life, processing, research, and similar values are left untouched
[/list]

As a result, cold weather generally has a clearer impact than in Vanilla / Medieval Overhaul, making crop choice, sowing time, forestry, heating, greenhouses, and preparation for cold snaps more important.

This is not a blanket nerf to every plant. Plants suited to overwintering can survive winter by entering dormancy instead of dying from ordinary cold.

[h2]Supported content[/h2]
[list]
[*]All 49 living Core PlantDefs in RimWorld 1.6
[*]All 29 Medieval Overhaul 1.6-specific PlantDefs when MO is installed
[*]Wild plants are included, not just cultivated crops
[*]Dead plant remnants such as stumps are excluded
[*]English / Japanese
[/list]

[h2]Planned support[/h2]

Support for [b]Vanilla Plants Expanded (VPE)[/b] is planned.

Because the full VPE plant catalog contains more than 100 plants, the first compatibility pass will target the [b]20 plants in the basic set[/b]. Support for additional VPE plants can be considered later based on priority and demand.

[h2]Framework for other mods[/h2]

CCTO is both a cold-tolerance rebalance and a [b]lightweight XML framework for other mods[/b].

Mods that add their own plants can assign fixed cold-death temperatures or cold dormancy through XML without replacing the Plant class.

[h2]Dependencies[/h2]
[b]Required:[/b] Harmony

[b]Optional:[/b] Medieval Overhaul / Nice Plants Menu / Dubs Mint Menus

[h2]Save compatibility[/h2]
[b]Safe to add to or remove from an existing save.[/b]

CCTO temperature settings apply to existing plants after loading. Plants already killed by cold are not restored by removing CCTO. If another mod requires CCTO, keep that dependency installed.
